Methods
Weekly water samples were collected from 3 sites and monthyl water samples from 4 sites, all within the Great Bay Watershed. The weekly sites included the Coastal Marine Lab (CML) in New Castle, NH, the Jackson Estuarine lab (JEL) in Durham, NH and Wiswall mill (WIS) in Durham, NH. The monthly sites included Hilton Park (HILT) in Dover, NH, Main street (MAIN) in Epping, NH, the Lamprey River Boat Launch (LAMP) in Epping, NH and Newmarket Boat Launch (NBL) in Newmarket, NH.
